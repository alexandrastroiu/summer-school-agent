# Getting around Milan

Milan's public transport is run by ATM and it's efficient, cheap and easy to use. The historic centre is compact enough to walk, but the metro is the fastest way to cross town, and a single ticket covers the metro, trams and buses across the urban area.

# Metro

The Milan Metro (Metropolitana di Milano) is Italy's longest metro network — 94.5 km of track and 113 stations across five colour-coded lines. Operated by ATM, it runs from 6am to 12:30am daily, and a single journey costs €2.20.

## Metro lines

- M1 (Red) — Sesto FS / Rho Fiera ↔ Bisceglie / Assago
- M2 (Green) — Cologno / Gessate ↔ Abbiategrasso / Assago
- M3 (Yellow) — Comasina ↔ San Donato
- M4 (Blue) — Linate Airport ↔ San Cristoforo
- M5 (Purple) — Bignami ↔ San Siro Stadio

## Ticket prices

ATM tickets are integrated — a single ticket is valid 90 minutes across metro, tram and bus, covering one metro journey plus unlimited surface transfers.
Buy at station vending machines, at newsagents and tobacconists (tabaccherie) with the ATM logo, or via the ATM Milano app.

## Fines

Validate before boarding. Tap your ticket at the yellow gate machines (or the on-board validator on trams and buses). Travelling without a validated ticket carries a €60–€100 fine, even with an unused ticket in your pocket.

## Hours

The metro runs daily from 6am to 12:30am. In central stations at peak times trains come every 2–4 minutes; at outer M2 terminals intervals can reach 30 minutes. On Saturday nights selected lines run until around 2am. On 25 December and 1 May the metro runs 7am–7:30pm only.

# Tram

## Key tram lines for tourists

- Line 1 – City centre loop
- Line 2 – Via Manzoni
- Line 9 – Centrale to Porta Genova
- Line 16 – Stazione Centrale
- Line 19 – Navigli & Sempione
- Line 24 – Brera to Porta Venezia

## Ticket prices

The standard ATM ticket is €2.20, valid 90 minutes on tram, bus and one metro journey. Validate at the yellow machine on board before the tram moves — buying from the driver adds a supplement.

## Hours

Trams run daily from about 6am to midnight. Frequency ranges from every 5 minutes on busy central routes to every 15–20 minutes on outlying lines. After midnight, night bus services (N lines) replace trams on most routes.

# Bus

Milan's bus network fills in where the metro and trams don't reach, with over 80 ATM urban lines plus a few trolleybus routes. The night bus network (N lines) keeps the city moving after midnight when the metro and trams stop. All buses use the standard ATM ticket (€2.20, valid 90 minutes).

## Key bus lines for tourists

- Bus 94 – City centre
- Night buses (N lines)

## Using the buses

Board at any door — Milan buses have no fixed boarding door — and validate your ticket immediately at the yellow machine on board

# Airport

Milano is served by 3 airports: Malpensa (MXP, 45 Km from the city centre), Linate (LIN, 7 Km from the city centre) and Bergamo Orio al Serio (BGY, 50 Km from the city centre).

The Malpensa Airport (MXP), about 45 Km from Milano has two operating terminals: T1 and T2, connected by a free shuttle bus service leaving about every 20 minutes.

The Linate (LIN) airport, about 7 Km from the city center, has now become a true city airport, mainly designed for frequent flyers towards national and international destinations inside the EU.

The Bergamo Orio al Serio (BGY) airport, about 50 Km from Milano, is located in the province of Bergamo and it is very important for low-cost flights.

# Getting to and from the airport

The Malpensa Express train from downtown Milan can take you to the airport. When it comes to buses and coaches, you can rely on the Malpensa Shuttle, Malpensa Airport Bus Express, Terravision or Caronte from Milano Centrale Station. If you want to hire a car at Milano Malpensa airport, you can find multiple car rental companies at the -1 level of both terminals. The rates for parking your own car at Malpensa start at €37 per day.

In order to reach Bergamo Airport, you can take the ATB bus, the Autostradale bus, the Terravision and the Orioshuttle. Car rental companies at the airport include Avis, Europcar and Hertz. Parking prices start at €15 a day.

You can reach Milano Linate Airport by train lines S5, S6 and S9. You can also take the M4 subway line. If you'd rather hire a car, you can do so in the Arrivals hall, where nine different rental companies have offices.
