# Food

Milan isn't only Italy's fashion capital — it's one of its most exciting cities for food. The traditional Milanese kitchen is rich, buttery and unmistakably northern, while the restaurant scene runs from neighbourhood trattorias to some of Italy's most creative contemporary kitchens.

## Where to eat? 

- Navigli: The liveliest area for evening dining and aperitivo, with canal-side bars and osterie along the Naviglio Grande.
- Brera: Elegant streets full of wine bars and upmarket trattorias — the choice for a refined dinner in a beautiful setting.
- Isola: Trendy and less touristy than Navigli or Brera — Milan's most interesting area for creative cooking, natural-wine bars and concept restaurants.
- Porta Ticinese: The best balance of price and quality: traditional trattorias and modern spots popular with students and young Milanese, just south of the centre.

## Restaurants in Milan

- Cantine Milano: Cantine Milano is a refined restaurant and wine bar that offers an extraordinary culinary experience.
- Come 'na Vorta - Pasta E Vino Milano:  Specializes in traditional Roman cuisine, offering dishes such as amatriciana, carbonara, gricia, polpette cacio e pepe, saltimbocca, fagioli con le cotiche, and pinsa.
-  Ristorante Borsieri 39: Ristorante Borsieri 39 is praised by many travelers for its authentic Milanese and Italian flavors, with generous portions and consistently high quality.
- Joia: Michelin-starred vegetarian restaurant.
- Anima: Awarded a Michelin star, ANIMA restaurant represents the excellence of contemporary gourmet cuisine.

# Shopping

- The Quadrilatero della Moda: The shopping area is bounded by four streets: Via Montenapoleone; Via Alessandro Manzoni; Via della Spiga and Corso Venecia. Here you will find amazing boutiques run by the leading fashion houses. Along with jewellers, shoe shops and eye-catching design studios. 
- Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II: Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is an undisputed highlight of the city of Milan. It is located in an area very popular with tourists, between the Duomo and the famous Teatro alla Scala. Here, you will find shops belonging to such outstanding names as Prada, Gucci and Louis Vuitton
- Corso Buenos Aires: Corso Buenos Aries is one of Milan’s most important shopping streets. It is in the north-west of the city, between the neighbourhoods of Centrale and Città Studi. It is somewhat outside the centre, but very easily accessible via Metro line 1. In contrast to the exclusivity of other streets such as Montenapoleone, this is a much more affordable area to shop in Milan. 
The area is known as outlet heaven, for both expensive names and other, more affordable brands. Outstanding examples include Benetton, Mandarina Duck, Nara Camicie, Timberland, Kookai, Zara, Stradivarius, Nike and Levi’s. The district has shops selling clothing, footwear and accessories, all at affordable prices.
